Support

Considered assistance for Travelers and Operators.

Contact

For Travel, payment, account, qualification or general questions, write to support@americanrider.app.

For Operator and business relationships, write to relations@americanrider.app.

For insurance coordination, use insurance@americanrider.app. Do not email identification documents, background reports, bank credentials or other sensitive records. Use the private in-app upload process or the authenticated channel requested by our team.

For an existing Travel, include your Travel number when available. Safety emergencies require local emergency services, not email.

Emergencies

Call 911. During every travel the app carries the operator's name, vehicle, license plate and the traveler's location, to be read to a dispatcher.

A travel

Patron Support opens from the menu or from the travel itself. It covers travel cost, a lost item, a route concern, a cancellation and safety.

Cases are settled against the record of the travel. Anything that should not be settled automatically, including any matter of safety, is directed to a person.

A lost item

A lost item is reported from the travel in the Travel Log. The operator who drove the travel is contacted first; where that operator has finished for the day, the return is dispatched to the nearest available operator.

Payment

Every travel carries a receipt in the Travel Log stating the fare, the platform fee and the total charged. A disputed amount is examined against the record of what was charged.

For a cancelled Travel, an unused card authorization is released or a captured amount is refunded as the original booking terms require. Cancellation fees are not automatic merely because an Operator arrived; any optional verified fee must first have been disclosed and accepted.

A Travel underway is resolved through Patron Support.

Operators

Payouts, qualification and insurance are handled on the operator side of the app, under Getting Paid and Documents.
