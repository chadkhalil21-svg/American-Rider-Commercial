Commercial insurance, screened criminal/sex-offender/driving history, documents and payouts remain mandatory **per Operator** before duty or dispatch. Applicants may choose any suitable screening firm, but American Rider verifies real agency evidence before approval. A synthetic test never qualifies an actual person. Prior blanket statements below about keeping the entire payment service closed pending one vendor pilot are superseded; the real intake/evidence and FCRA requirements still apply to individual cases.

## Architecture and limitations observed
- The app at `app/operator/background.tsx` offers a provider URL (when configured) and separate **existing-report** and **new-screening** requests. That request opens a case only: an Operator declaration **never** approves them.
- `backend/screening.js` contains Florida screening adjudication, operator suspension, and 3-year expiry. Live qualification requires a real, current `pass`, not an onboarding checkbox.
- `backend/checkr.js` contains an old optional, provider-specific adapter, **but no active Checkr webhook is mounted in the current server**. Do not claim a Checkr pipeline is live merely because code or an API key exists.
- A vendor-neutral manual intake path is now present: Operators authorize provider-to-American Rider delivery and create a case; named Operations staff use `/ops/screening` to attest to independent verification and the required searches. `backend/external-screening.js` rejects any case that is not open, tied to that Operator, and backed by their prior written request. Decisions and audit entries are written in one Firestore transaction; the report itself is **not** stored in Firestore. **A staff attestation cannot automatically establish that the provider really delivered valid evidence.** The record must be checked directly with the agency using a secure channel.
- Production `screeningReady()` requires `SCREENING_EVIDENCE_WORKFLOW_VERIFIED=true` and does **not** require a provider URL, a release sign-off set **only after** demonstrated evidence. The flag is an owner attestation, not an automated independent verification. **Leave it unset/false until the whole process has been demonstrated.**
- All production matching calls demand screening independently of that setting. A missing provider must never weaken eligibility.

## Commissioning checklist — required before flipping readiness
1. **Source:** Operator chooses a qualified CRA, or American Rider offers a nonexclusive referral list. American Rider confirms the selected source is appropriate for the Florida TNC checks. Confirm the permitted purpose, written contract, supported states, operator-paid vs company-billed policy, consent, and data processing/retention. Do **not** promise operator-direct payment until the provider confirms it.
2. **Package:** Obtain documented proof the CRA can perform: (a) a local/national criminal background search including commercially sourced nationwide database and primary source validation, (b) the DOJ national sex-offender database search, and (c) a driving history research report. Map disqualifiers and repeat every 3 years as required by Florida statute. Historical checks from other purposes require fresh CRA permission/provenance and a documented legal review—not automatic transfer.
3. **Intake:** Use the existing authenticated staff-only case review with direct source authentication in the agency's portal or verified secure transfer. The staff attestation must be based on actual agency evidence, **not** a checkbox alone. Never accept a screenshot, user assertion, arbitrary webhook POST, or forwarded consumer copy as dispositive. Minimize PII and restrict access/retention.
4. **Adjudication and exceptions:** Verify source identity, operator identity, report issuance date, all required components, moving violations, unresolved/ambiguous records, individualized review and legally compliant notice/adverse-action sequence. Unreadable or disputed records stay **on hold**, never pass. Audit who acted and why.
5. **Realistic pilot:** With provider-issued staging or owner-approved test cases, demonstrate invitation/receipt or consented report transfer, vendor-signed delivery, full clear result, refusal/hold, adverse notice and dispute, retries/deduplication, wrong-operator isolation, and three-year expiration. Prove a current PASS on the server gates an actual eligible operator, and unscreened operators cannot go on duty or accept paid Travel. Keep the market on waitlist while testing.
6. **Release evidence:** Record accepted provider standards, permitted-use arrangements, proof of required searches, reporting costs, secure evidence access, verification audit, adverse-action policy and production QA date. Only after an authorized pilot passes should the owner set `SCREENING_EVIDENCE_WORKFLOW_VERIFIED=true` in Render. `SCREENING_PROVIDER_URL` is optional and solely supplies a provider referral link. Confirm `/health`, `/ops`, physical-device onboarding and a current, authenticated PASS before activating any market. **Do not enable the sign-off merely to clear `operationalMissing`.**

## Safeguards and outstanding human approvals
- An Operator-uploaded report or a declaration never auto-passes. The person reviewing must authenticate the report directly with the CRA, verify lawful purpose/transfer, and explicitly attest to Florida’s criminal-source validation, sex-offender search, current license, registration and driving record. A provider’s ordinary consumer-facing personal report may **not** be reusable for this purpose.
- A `hold` leaves the case open and the Operator blocked; an audited `clear` closes only the corresponding screening case and **does not commence operations**. All operator eligibility gates remain in force.
- No screening payment is collected by American Rider through this workflow. Operators should **not** spend money on a report until the selected agency confirms it is suitable for TNC use and can transmit it lawfully to American Rider. Some providers charge the requesting TNC instead of the Operator; never represent direct payment as guaranteed.
- The Operations form must not contain SSN, birth date, record narratives or consumer reports. Arrange confidential access directly with the CRA. The case email is for coordinating a secure transfer, **not** attaching a sensitive report.
- An Operations employee attestation and technical tests are not a substitute for counsel’s review of legal re-use, consumer reporting, and adverse-action notifications. Review the FTC/FCRA requirements before live clearances: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/background-checks-what-employers-need-know.
