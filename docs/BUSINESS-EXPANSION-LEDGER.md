## 30 October 2026 — Social-media creator and influencer Recruiter channel

**Founder directive:** American Rider can approach relevant social-media creators, local community voices and influencers and offer them participation in **the existing independent Recruiter referral program**. This is a possible acquisition channel, not an authorization to begin outreach today.

**Approved concept for future execution:**
- Target creators whose audiences could become genuine Travelers or qualified Operators, with local relevance in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach at launch. Examples of channels: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, and Spanish/English local communities. Do not assume follower counts equal local customers.
- Invite creators as **independent, commission-only Recruiters** through the existing Founder-approved private invitation/acceptance flow. No special influencer tier, new subscription, salary, hourly ambassador program, or upfront advertisement fee is proposed.
- Use each Recruiter's unique account-linked code and separate Traveler and Operator referral links / QR codes, preserving original attribution and the existing payout controls.
- Preserve the **existing commission** without changes: **$0.15 per qualifying completed, paid and settled Travel attributable to a recruited Traveler OR Operator**, capped at **200 qualifying Travels per recruited participant/role**, thus **$30 maximum per recruited person/role**. If two different Recruiters legitimately recruited the Traveler and Operator for the same eligible Travel, total program payout may be **$0.30** under the already approved two-sided rule. Registration, clicks, impressions, unfinished or refunded Travels do **not** generate earned commission.
- Payment remains subject to the existing agreement, eligibility, fraud/duplicate/self-referral checks, reversals, tax/banking identity requirements and Stripe Connect payout rules; preserve previously approved, disclosed $2 active-payout-month fee where applicable. No live payout or successful production activation is implied by this planning record.
- Require clear and prominent disclosure of the material referral relationship on posts/videos in accordance with applicable advertising rules; avoid misleading earnings, fare, insurance or availability claims and require permission before using other people's identities or content.
- Measure unique link visits → verified account registrations → eligible Operator qualifications / first completed Traveler Travels → settled Travels → net acquisition cost and repeat usage. Use cohort reporting, caps, geographic supply matching and fraud monitoring. Avoid paying for downloads or unverified leads.

**Reasoning:** Unlike hourly brand ambassadors or fixed-price influencer campaigns, a Recruiter arrangement ties cost to valid commercial activity, with a known maximum commission per legitimately recruited participant. It does **not** guarantee creator participation, Traveler supply, or successful economics.

**State:** PLANNING ONLY. No creator has been contacted, invited, approved or offered compensation through this ledger change. No production system, referral rate, payment configuration or marketing spend is changed.
