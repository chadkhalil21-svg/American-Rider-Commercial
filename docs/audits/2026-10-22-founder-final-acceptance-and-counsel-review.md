## Engineering decisions from published guidance
- **Google Firestore:** retain indexed, bounded, cursor-based case/Operator discovery; avoid Firestore offsets and unused index fanout. Actual billed reads and Query Explain are not independently measured. https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/best-practices
- **Uber H3:** H3 is a viable hierarchical hexagonal index; **do not migrate** simply because Uber uses it. Current `backend/geooperators.js` uses bounded geohash neighborhood queries plus the existing exact-distance/eligibility matcher. Match quality, Firestore index/dual-write migration and cost must be benchmarked first. Hexagonal cell membership itself is **not** an eligibility or distance decision. https://h3geo.org/docs/
- **Google SRE:** actionable, idempotent exceptions in the existing Operations queue are preferred to duplicate alerts and another periodic scanner. https://sre.google/workbook/alerting-on-slos/
- **Stripe:** `requires_capture` is an authorization; capture, cancellation, and refund require separate provider-state reconciliation. Connected-account transfers and bank payouts are separate settlement steps. https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method
- **Apple / Google:** an exported JS bundle or a green CI test is not native Apple Pay, Google Pay, 3DS or physical device acceptance. Apple Pay requires the proper merchant setup and on-device sandbox tests; Google Pay requires functional testing and production approval where applicable. https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/sandbox-testing/ and https://developers.google.com/pay/api/android/guides/test-and-deploy/integration-checklist

## Unverified, not source regressions
- One eligible and independently verified insurance/screening Operator, a second signed-in Traveler device, pickup identity/handoff, Teen verification where relevant and destination arrival on the actual signed app.
- Native Apple Pay/Google Pay payment confirmation and step-up/3DS challenge on physical devices and current binary. No new TestFlight may be created or sent absent the Founder's new explicit authorization.
- Exact Travel complete → held PI capture → Stripe Connect transfer **from that original charge** → standard/Instant payout availability or bank arrival and relevant fee disclosure. Prior Stripe TEST PI cases and a historical sandbox transfer are **not** this whole acceptance.
- Live negative cases: payment decline, expired hold, offline reconnect, no-Operator dispatch, cancellation before charge and disputed capture; demonstrate no duplicate charge, refund or transfer.
- **Florida legal counsel checkpoint, real issue:** §627.748(7)(d) says that if the driver's insurance has lapsed or lacks required coverage, insurance maintained by the TNC must provide required coverage from the first dollar and defend. Other subsections prescribe $1 million while carrying a Traveler, logged-on coverage, disclosures, zero-tolerance and licensing. The Founder decided not to buy a blanket TNC policy, but **software refusal to dispatch an ineligible Operator is not proven to replace the statutory TNC-insurer backstop for a lapse**. A qualified Florida TNC coverage attorney and licensed insurance professional must specifically review this before any claim that uninsured platform launch is compliant. This is an issue for actual licensed counsel, **not** an assertion that such counsel has been consulted. Primary source: https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0627/Sections/0627.748.html
