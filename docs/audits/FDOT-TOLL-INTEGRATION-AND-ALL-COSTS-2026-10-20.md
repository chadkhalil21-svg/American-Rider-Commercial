## Exact government sources and concrete integration
1. **Download real Florida toll-road geometry** as GeoJSON from FDOT's state road inventory ArcGIS service, layer `RCI_Layers/FeatureServer/24` (fields include road name, toll flag, county and location): https://gis.fdot.gov/arcgis/rest/services/RCI_Layers/FeatureServer/24 . Run `node scripts/fdot-toll-road-inventory.cjs --output /tmp/fl-tolls-review.json` from an environment with public internet. The new script does bounded, read-only, paginated HTTPS requests (500 features/page), validates feature types, records retrieval timestamp and content SHA256, writes with exclusive-create semantics and refuses partial/malformed downloads. This is an **offline review dataset**, NO server secret, Google account, new subscription or fare mutation.
2. **Extract OSM roads and toll gantries** from the licensed region OSM extract already feeding OSRM. OSM exposes `toll=yes`, `highway=toll_gantry`, occasional `charge=*` tags; FDOT supplies an independent toll-road coverage crosscheck. Map each actual OSRM route edge to official gantry crossing geometry with direction and repeat-capture avoidance. All Florida facilities and dynamic express lanes must be reviewed, and the matching must prove full coverage before marking `officialMatchComplete=true`. OSM coverage quality varies; mapping missing/negative is UNKNOWN, not zero. Offline reviewer approves coverage by market and timestamp, not the client.
3. **Download/approve actual dollar rates** separately. Florida Turnpike's public rates and calculator: https://floridasturnpike.com/tolls/toll-rates/ ; importantly, a newer 2025 Florida official rule-linked rate PDF exists: https://www.floridasturnpike.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/14-15.0081-Toll-Rate-Schedule_2-25-2025.pdf . Do NOT blindly copy the homepage's 2023 effective-date table. Compare current effective dates, toll location/entry-exit/direction, vehicle axle count, accepted SunPass account and toll-by-plate, and add separately verified GMX/express-lane rates and permissions. Variable-priced facilities need valid current official feed or a licensed HERE exact-route fallback, never a frozen PDF price.
4. The existing `backend/official-toll-pricing.js` expects `route.officialFacilityCrossings`, `officialMatchSource`, `officialMatchComplete`, certified vehicle/payment context, and `APPROVED_OFFICIAL_RATE_BOOKS`. **These values remain uncommissioned/empty in the actual release; the new GIS script does not claim otherwise.** After independent versioned approval, an offline mapping worker can attach these fields to server-generated OSRM routes and resolve from local rate snapshots at near-zero per-Travel vendor cost. Unknown/mismatched/expired -> HERE fallback on one route, else UNKNOWN (not zero). Test at least 100 route cases against official calculators, different times/payment types and near toll-free alternatives before billing.
5. **SunPass and other regional tags:** enroll Operator actual toll-payment capability `none|verified_transponder|plate` with jurisdictional `system` (SunPass, E-ZPass etc), vehicle plate, class/axles, effective dates and evidence/verification; do not automatically trust an uploaded declaration. BEFORE matching an Operator we cannot truthfully assume whose SunPass will pay the toll; design must either use a disclosed conservative quote followed by an explicit agreed final adjustment or avoid tolled routes until matching, not silently charge different amounts. Operator receives 100% of genuinely incurred toll. Official state policy: https://floridasturnpike.com/tolls/sunpass/ . HERE fare methods are alternatives (not additive), and toll add-on requests are separately billable: https://docs.here.com/routing/docs/routing-v8-tolls-transponders .
6. HERE base self-service license excludes certain asset management; verify right to ridehail/taxi-like app service (search, routing, fleet tracking) with HERE in writing. https://www.here.com/get-started/pricing/base-plan-restrictions . No licensed-attorney consultation has occurred.

## Photon without unnecessary expense

Deploy no Photon instance until the **actual** HERE account billed cost, pricing rights, full quality benchmark, steady-use traffic and ROI show a gain.
