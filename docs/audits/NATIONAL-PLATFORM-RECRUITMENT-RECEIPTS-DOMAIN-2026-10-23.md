## Evidence observed directly in `release/current`
- `backend/email.js` renders branded Traveler/Operator email and `backend/server.js` invokes the receipt path when settlement occurs. `backend/travelmoney.js::settleTravel` can return early on `transferId`, and stores `receiptFailed` on provider failure. This is **not** durable exactly-once, provider-independent receipt completion: no verified bounded retry outbox, concurrent claim, delivery event reconciliation, or replay across more than Resend's 24-hour idempotency window. On payment-account failure, it can also return before the receipt attempt. Receipt generation must follow VERIFIED captured payment independently of whether Operator bank transfer succeeds, and recover without double emailing.
- `backend/email.js` had default receipt sender and Support correspondence at `@americanrider.app`; `backend/server.js` includes direct `insurance@americanrider.app` overrides, `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` and `CORS_ORIGINS` defaulting to `.app`. These require **separate** migration control, not blind removal. `backend/referral-policy.js` and its tests still generate `https://americanrider.app/join?... `. `americanrider.tech/join/` is currently informational and does not prove universal/app-link ownership or retention of attribution.
- `backend/recruiter-approval.js`: `PROGRAM_ENABLED=false`, module not mounted as live approved-recruiter endpoint; `backend/referral-service.js` basic role-code sharing/first claim exists, separately guarded by `AR_REFERRALS_ENABLED`; basic attribution sets `rewardEnabled:false`.

- Public `/recruitment/` route accepts preliminary email inquiries only. Full recruiter onboarding is **not** accomplished by that page. Mobile `Invite a Traveler` remains a distinct unpaid product pathway; do not contaminate its hospitality tone or existing screen.

## Target end-to-end architecture | One canonical identity and one service
1. **Founder invitation and contract:** Private MFA-scoped founder action issues a single-use, email-bound, short-expiry invitation to a verified recipient. Never accept an emailed text instruction as founder authorization. Professionally worded message from `relations@`; email bounce/revoke/resend, expiry and audit record.
2. **Recruiter onboarding (secure, store-safe):** Link opens verified app/universal link with web fallback; login/SSO and confirmed recipient email; identity, tax disclosures, program agreement, prohibited claims and commission disclosure. Counsel reviews independent contractor versus employee status and any state marketing/solicitation rules. Account can be suspended/revoked immediately without affecting ordinary Traveler/Operator accounts.
3. **Recruiter workspace:** Calm role-specific section reachable on iOS/Android, or an authenticated responsive web view if app review and permissions permit. Screen: “Introductions”; separate verified Operators, pending Operators, referred Travelers, qualified Travels, pending/held earnings, available balance, payouts and adjustments. Only own approved records, server-derived aggregate counts, no public personally identifying information and no auto-access to applicant screening, policies or another recruiter's numbers. Filter by date and region. No unverified hypothetical earnings.
4. **Unique referral identity:** Server-created opaque URL+QR, immutable ownership binding and role-based first accepted attribution; share sheet, recovery after install/login, no trust of `uid` or amounts passed in URL. First-day role, stored attribution and repeat/cross-device tested. Carefully preserve old `.app` link resolution until `.tech` verified for both iOS Universal Links/Android App Links on real signed builds. Reject related/self-referral, replayed, revoked and expired links.
5. **Qualifying introductions:** Referred Operator counts only after a verified eligible account in an actually opened geography; acquisition from unopened geography should display “Not yet available” without claiming commissions. Track interest separately. Never infer legal qualification from H3/ZIP/county aggregates. Optionally use Uber H3 for *coarse aggregated* geographically grouped recruitment reports, not licensing or personal identity.
6. **Money and source of truth:** Paid/captured, completed, no disputed/refunded/synthetic/chargeback-troubled Travel before any qualification. Server-side immutable `(recruiter, role, event, program-version)` ledger, idempotent accrual and reversal; distinguish credited, held, available, paid, returned and adjusted states. Conservation of funds, sustainable fee economics before offer. No reward accrual from click, signup, screenshot or unverified “completed” status.
7. **Bank/tax onboarding:** Never collect routing number, account number, SSN or tax-ID through email or store raw bank credentials in our own app. Choose a compliant provider-approved hosted payout/tax-collection flow, return provider recipient reference/token and only last-four/status. Require provider verification, identity checks where applicable, signer agreement, access revocation and minimum funded threshold. Review reporting ownership and 2026 US tax thresholds. Treat `1099-NEC` qualification separately from whether a processor files a `1099-K`.
8. **Payment:** Independently reviewed ACH provider, pre-funded commission ledger, founder-approved schedule and pay threshold, reserve, immutable batch ID, at-most-once bank initiation, reconciliation of accepted/failed/returned/transferred events, founder exceptions, downloadable statement and notice from `notifications@`. **Never assume Friday or $25 threshold is approved.**
9. **Receipt email:** Following verified capture, persist a versioned, immutable receipt snapshot, queue separate delivery with stable event identity and retry-later policy, transactional outbox claim with lease, vendor idempotency for near-term retries, bounded backoff/429 handling, replay after worker crash, provider send/delivered/bounce/complaint webhooks, dedupe beyond 24-hour vendor window, and an in-app downloadable receipt regardless of email delivery. Receipt replies route to `support@`. Refund/adjustment notices must be their own ledger events and not mutate an old paid receipt.
10. **Separate service types:** Account verification/reset (Firebase Auth); real payment receipt Stripe where supported; custom Travel itinerary receipts, Operator settlement and recruiter messages through controlled transactional email. Manage provider-agnostic mail adapter and volume meter, not Gmail bulk sends.
11. **App publishing:** Production signed Apple+Google builds, invite/deep-link install/reopen on real devices, reviewer demo identities for Traveler/Operator/Recruiter plus store listing/privacy/data deletion. Full purchase and money traces using both synthetic sandbox and independent live provider evidence; accurate coverage and disabled Smart Travel. App Store approval is independent of code passing CI.
12. **Operating controls:** One founder action for suspend/approve; event-driven status, payout/receipt exception alert only, rate limits, quotas, retries and backoff. No manual full-network sweeps or giant constant polling job. Provide a private audit ledger and exportable quarterly payments/reporting facts with minimum data necessary.

## New October 22 security advisory discovered by exact-head release checks
GitHub Actions `npm audit --omit=dev --prefix backend` blocked this review because the existing transitive Express package `body-parser` was locked at **1.20.8**. Published **CVE-2026-111181 / GHSA-27r3-jxmh-p828** (high severity) describes an unauthenticated process crash involving trailing bytes on compressed requests; patch available at **1.20.9**. Source: https://github.com/expressjs/body-parser/security/advisories/GHSA-27r3-jxmh-p828 . This defect predated this review's receipt changes, but is a real backend release blocker. The guarded PR now pins the patched `body-parser` version via an explicit dependency override and matching verified lockfile tarball SHA-512 (checked across multiple independent current public lockfiles). **Do not merge until exact-head npm audit and whole-platform CI are GREEN**. A GitHub change and dependency audit are still not independent runtime penetration-test evidence.
