## Funnel forecast, not published industry fact
No sufficiently comparable research was found that publishes **American Rider-specific** destination-search-session to *completed paid ride* conversions. Travel-hotel cart/visitor conversions are not valid direct proxies. Peer-reviewed ridehail studies verify abandonment/driver supply are real, not a universal conversion ratio: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02346 ; Baymard research emphasizes search and price/checkout friction https://baymard.com/research-articles/new-research-travel-accommodations . No genuine proprietary operator/Traveler historical count is available. Do not advertise 25% as an industry average.

Define conversion = **completed charged Travels / distinct intentful destination-search sessions**; not per keystroke. Count repeated price checks, unsuccessful requests, failures and searches without booking. Planning bands **5% severe launch stress; 10% supply-constrained launch stress; 25% provisional mature central hypothesis; 40% optimistic repeat-user scenario**. None is a measured probability. Suggested booking *volume* scenarios (monthly, not predictions): pilot month 100, next months 1,000, growth 10,000, regional scale 100,000 and national scale 1,000,000. Monthly volume is bounded by online Operator availability, completed/travel timing, acquisition and retention. Do not claim a precise first-month forecast from vendor API price tables.

USD-only **illustrative uncontracted** provider planning inputs: five provider Autosuggest calls PER session after client debounce (350ms) and 0.5 toll quote per session; $3 per 1,000 Autosuggest requests, $1 per 1,000 standard route requests and **$5 additional per 1,000 toll-cost transactions**. These are sensitivity knobs, deliberately NOT statements of contracted HERE rates. Each session cost = (5×$3 + 0.5×($1+$5))/1000 = **$0.018**. If a quoted journey retries or additional selected routes are sought, toll requests may be higher. Shared cache may lower Autosuggest use, but no production reduction is assumed.

## Photon and alternate hosting
Photon is open-source software ($0 software license), NOT free always-on compute: Java 21+, persistent OpenStreetMap searchable index, RAM, current updates, traffic/retries, persistent storage and availability. The ~10.5 GB USA compressed archive is not a standalone HTTP autocomplete endpoint. Upstream: https://github.com/komoot/photon . ADR0041 estimated GCP 2 vCPU/8 GiB VM $48.92/month plus ~150 GiB balanced disk $15/month, **~$64/month** before networking, IP, backup, operations, failures, traffic. An AWS Lightsail Linux instance **8 GiB RAM, 160 GiB disk** lists **$44/month** with public IPv4 (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/lightsail/latest/userguide/amazon-lightsail-bundles.html), but cross-cloud network/private connection, availability and latency costs must be added. A 4 GiB/80 GiB Lightsail instance lists $24/month but 80 GiB may not suffice depending on extracted U.S. dataset; no production quality/capacity certification. Existing Render Starter has only 512 MiB RAM and cannot host the Photon index as proposed. Do not create new account/resource without founder approval; compare measured HERE account usage and the need for real uptime first.

## Toll/SunPass account matching and P0
Florida Turnpike states SunPass usually saves **~25%** versus TOLL-BY-PLATE. Electronic toll lanes can require an accepted transponder, and plate fees/administrative surcharges differ. Sources: https://floridasturnpike.com/tolls/sunpass/ ; https://floridasturnpike.com/tolls/toll-by-plate/ .
**Future policy approval needed:** Operator's plate and **verified** transponder type / active status and vehicle axle class at onboarding, with secure updates; no declaration alone as guaranteed verification; effective applicable tariffs from official source or HERE where licensed. Current pre-match quote often cannot know WHICH Operator's SunPass/plate account will carry the Traveler, so do not invent one as certain. Any final reconciliation/change must be disclosed and authorized before charging; preserve actual Operator 100% toll pass-through and Traveler price consent.
HERE toll API's fare list can represent alternative payments, not additive costs; HERE `return=tolls` adds another priced transaction: https://docs.here.com/routing/docs/routing-v8-tolls-for-route ; https://docs.here.com/routing/docs/routing-v8-tolls-transponders . Existing parser summed unique alternate IDs and might overquote. Branch `fix/here-toll-fare-integrity-20261020` adds ambiguity refusal and blocks separately calculated HERE toll amount for an unmatched OSRM/OTP route. This is a **fail-closed source candidate**, may increase toll-UNKNOWN refusal in markets without commissioned verified official rates; DO NOT deploy without controlled functional booking/quote tests. Tests are synthetic; production has not been altered. This does not yet implement end-to-end SunPass credential/toll identity for unmatched Operator quotes.

## Minimum-cost action order
1. HERE account-specific per-SKU usage + invoices, allowed commercial taxi scope, subscription/add-ons and account-wide quotas; preserve key confidentiality.
2. Privacy-safe service-wide counters: search sessions → provider requests → quote attempts → completed charged Travels, no-booking events, source/latency, true HERE charge, HERE-toll unknown %, verified settlement deviations; do NOT store full typed addresses in central metrics.
3. Implement provider-native account-level spend caps and application limits. Keep in-app debouncing, cancellation, in-flight coalescing and 10-min cache. Do not allow provider outage to cause thousands of retry calls.
4. Complete/field-test toll mapping and payment policy: official gantry/rate books, exact selected road route and Operator payment credentials; no invented $0 or inflated summed fares.
5. Compare HERE monthly **billed** amount and quality to commissioned trial Photon, with official benchmark inputs on dozens of duplicate POIs, rural/airport addresses, 5 languages, national search and true route conversion.
6. Only switch primary from currently working HERE after signed-device proof and vendor contractual signoff; deploy through reviewed CI and controlled Cloud Run release. No extra supplier signup by default.
