## Exactly who sets bank payout timing
Stripe Connect distinguishes:
1. **Travel completion transfer:** American Rider already creates a `source_transaction` Stripe transfer for 99% fare + tolls after Travel completes. This is NOT a bank deposit.
2. **Ordinary bank payout:** Stripe later sends Connect balance to the Operator's bank. Stripe publishes automatic schedules `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly` or `manual`, with daily rolling the default if not overridden. For our Express accounts the **platform controls the automatic schedule**, not a fixed four-Friday calendar. The app currently links to Stripe Express and does NOT call `payouts.create` or `payouts.update` to impose a weekly payday. Actual fresh Express account defaults cannot be independently verified without creating one in Stripe sandbox. Keep the default automatic ordinary bank payout to maximize convenience, subject to Stripe country/industry restrictions.
3. **Instant payout:** Operator opts in when eligibility/availability permits; 1% additional Connect fee under published US model; enable explicit visible price and Stripe-supported embedded/Express flow before promising operator-initiated instant. Stripe says the platform can offer an embedded instant-payout component or API; availability limits apply. No app-owned manual payout scheduler for day-to-day use.
A pure **manual-only** scheme would require us to trigger ordinary payouts through Stripe's API, comply with time limits and provide a self-service interface; it is not equivalent to simply turning off schedules.

## Actual public Stripe fees, not an account invoice
Reference https://stripe.com/connect/pricing and https://docs.stripe.com/connect/payouts-connected-accounts .
A. **Platform-handled pricing** (historically Express-like): $2 for bank-payout-active account each month, 0.25% + $0.25 per external payout, separate 0.25% of bank payout volume for routing/platform management, optional additional 1% of Instant Payout volume. A $100 single standard bank payout in one month: $2 + $0.50 + $0.25 = $2.75; optional instant +$1 = $3.75. Two $100 payouts cost $2 +2×($0.50+$0.25) = $3.50, so high frequency increases fixed 25c payout events. **Standard is not inherently free** here, only without 1% instant fee.
B. **Stripe-handled connected-account pricing:** Stripe advertises no additional account, ordinary bank payout and funds-routing charges to platform but instead directly handles connected-account pricing. Do not assume this option is compatible with our Express configuration and platform-merchant-of-record `separate charges and transfers` model without explicit product/account confirmation; no secret plan read from Stripe API proved compatibility.
C. **Stripe connected balance account debit:** documented for connected US accounts where the platform is liable for negative balances with legally binding consent, enough balance and **extra 1.5% of debit volume** (3c on $2), https://docs.stripe.com/connect/account-debits . Avoid recurring separate saved cards; however funds already paid to the bank cannot be collected from an empty balance. Fee invoice and failed collection must roll forward against future earnings as expressly agreed, or be written off within a defined company policy. Do not overdraw or withhold earned proceeds illegally.
D. **Net authorized fees from future Travel earnings:** Stripe documents collecting a marketplace fee by reducing the separate transfer amount to a connected account, https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers . Gross 99% remains earned/contractual; show authorized Stripe fee obligations separately. Deduct only VERIFIED due obligations (same Operator, exact month, payout ID, rates, amount) and not estimated future monthly charges. Fee incurred AFTER a bank payout might be collectible only from FUTURE gross earnings, unless a separate balance debit with authorized consent succeeds. No artificial weekly holding period just to avoid 25c.

## Firestore expense reduction
Current `backend/operatorfees.js:sweepOperatorAccountFees` reads ALL previous-month account docs every six hours, including `paid`/`waived`; remote Stripe card attempts add network calls and writes. New source guard avoids these scans entirely until safe collection enabled. After full balance recovery is commissioned, process each payout as one signed webhook into a durable single obligation document keyed by (connected account, payout ID) and month, update only changed records, and query `status=due AND nextAttemptAt<=now` with bounded indexed pages rather than reading all historical paid records. Backfill legacy records to avoid silently dropping nonindexed statuses. Batch payment reconciliation and only write status changes. Use Firestore Query Explain on real data to measure read+index bills; no broad index removal without query proof. Existing geohash cursor paging, duty optimization and stranded-assignment write suppression are already merged; do NOT redo.

## Google / Uber H3 / Stripe engineering guidance and concrete read/write response

- Existing six-hour Operator fee sweep would read **every account document from the previous month on every cycle**. A 30-day month with a due prior month and 4 sweeps/day produces as many as **120 reads/account/month** before other queries; a hypothetical 100,000 previously-active Operators means up to 12 million monthly scanned records, before adding the user docs. Actual costs depend on active records and billing; this is a *read count upper illustration*, not observed cloud usage. PR safety correction **stops the scan while billing disabled**. Final service must instead use verified `payout.*` events, one monthly record, a durable `feeStatus=due,nextAttemptAt<=now` queue with bounded index-backed pages, idempotency & legacy backfill. Never suspend provider queue, legitimate payout webhooks, insurance or urgent SOS safety checks.
- Google recommends minimizing Firestore index fanout, unnecessary descending/array indexes, and nonqueried sequential/TTL field indexes; do not indiscriminately remove indexes from collections with live query requirements: https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/best-practices . Use Firestore Query Explain to compare actual billed docs/index entries; 1 planning read is still billed for a default explain: https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/query-data/explain
- Uber's H3 provides hierarchical hexagonal spatial indexing, useful for aggregating geography but not automatically faster or less expensive than the currently merged geohash dispatch. H3 is NOT a map provider, geocoder, payout tool or billing optimization. Benchmark geohash against H3 with the same geographically distributed synthetic Operators and production-like workloads (urban/county borders, 1K, 10K, 100K online Operators), measure candidate recall, indexed reads, extra index writes and p95 latency. Only migrate if net gain is proved and dispatch eligibility still safe: https://h3geo.org/docs/ and https://h3geo.org/docs/comparisons/geohash/
- Control internal service costs by paying the *real* Stripe account invoice, not double collecting developer-estimated charges. Stripe documents separate charges/transfers fee responsibility and multiple pricing arrangements: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/charges and https://stripe.com/connect/pricing .
