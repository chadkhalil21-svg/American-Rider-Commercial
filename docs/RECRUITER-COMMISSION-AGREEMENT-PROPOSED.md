## 1. Program and eligibility
American Rider may approve an individual to introduce prospective Operators and Travelers. Independent Recruiters use the American Rider website. An existing Operator may refer through their current American Rider account; they do **not** need a separate Recruiter dashboard or a second Stripe Connect account. Payment requires real identification and tax review, an approved referral relationship, appropriate consent and good-standing Stripe onboarding. Sharing a URL does not by itself earn anything.

## 2. Earned commission (Founder-approved structure)
For each eligible completed, paid and settled Travel involving a new person attributed to the Recruiter through a valid role-specific invitation:
- **$0.15** for a referred Operator, up to **200 qualifying Travels per referred Operator** ($30 gross maximum for that person).
- **$0.15** for a referred Traveler, up to **200 qualifying Travels per referred Traveler** ($30 gross maximum for that person).
- If both sides of one eligible Travel were introduced, **both $0.15 commissions** may be earned, even when attributed to different Recruiters. Maximum total referral expense on that Travel: $0.30.
- Multiple independently introduced people can contribute to one Recruiter's earnings; the 200-Travel limit is **per introduced identity and role**, not per Recruiter.

Commissions come from American Rider's pre-verified platform contribution and never reduce the Operator's **99% of the Travel Fare** or silently increase the Traveler's final price. Duplicate, fictitious, self-referred or collusive booking activity earns nothing. Refunds, disputes, fraud or reversals may cancel or recover commissions under a disclosed documented adjustment procedure. When previously paid funds need recovery, the platform must retain an auditable claim and must not silently take unrelated Operator Travel earnings.

## 3. Settlement and payouts (FOUNDER APPROVED; legal review and implementation acceptance pending)
A commission becomes eligible for payment only **24 hours after the Travel's qualifying paid settlement and recorded accrual**, and only when any fraud, refund, chargeback or provider-risk check is satisfied. This is not an extra fee and does not promise a transfer after exactly 24 hours. Only available, settled and unreversed commissions qualify. Additional lawful holds require recorded grounds and review.

**Ordinary payout:** aggregate matured available earnings across all referred persons. Check and release eligible balances **once per calendar month, ordinarily at the next UTC month boundary**, provided the verified recipient's gross available balance is at least **$25**. A commission first reaching $25 does **not** trigger an immediate mid-month bank transfer. The actual bank arrival date depends on Stripe and financial institutions. $25 is a batching threshold, not a fee or cap on lifetime earnings.

**Residual closeout to avoid stranded earnings (Founder approved):** after the Recruiter terminates the relationship, or after **90 consecutive days without new qualifying activity**, make matured, undisputed available earnings eligible for closeout even below the usual $25 threshold, subject to verified beneficiary, lawful payout permissions and documented settlement processing. Do not pay disputed/suspended or otherwise prohibited funds automatically. If a standard $2 active-month fee would consume the residual, American Rider would absorb or waive that fee on this final closeout rather than forfeit an earned commission. A standalone U.S. Stripe bank payout generally cannot be initiated below $1; such smaller earned balances remain obligations of American Rider for an alternative lawful final settlement and **must not be forfeited or sent to a Stripe account that cannot pay them out**. The method and time limit for sub-provider-minimum residuals, and for termination of a disputed or suspended account, require a documented legally compliant closeout procedure. Never silently expire small earned balances or bypass applicable unclaimed-property, inactive-account or payment restrictions. Failed/bank-returned payments remain owed, held and traceable until a valid recipient or lawful alternative is available.

## 4. Provider fees (Founder approved; final legal/provider disclosure review pending)
American Rider's Stripe Connect configuration presently prices active connected accounts at **$2 per calendar month with a successful bank payout**, plus provider processing charges. For an independent Recruiter, the agreed **$2 fee** is deducted **once per payout-active month** from that Recruiter's earned commissions; it is not charged for referral signups, link sharing, holding balances or every Travel. American Rider absorbs standard payout-processing charges.

**Existing Operator:** use their existing verified Stripe Connect account and its established bank payout schedule. **Do not open a second Stripe Connect account or charge a second $2 active-account fee.** A commission transfer into the Operator's Stripe balance must be distinguishable in the ledger from a subsequently completed bank payout. Existing Operator fee consent, extra tax reporting, refunds and chargebacks require separate validation before the first paid referral is routed. Do not change their ordinary payout schedule.

Illustration: a single introduced person whose 200 qualifying Travels all earn $0.15 generates **$30 gross**. If one independent Recruiter payout occurs in a month subject to the $2 fee, they receive **$28 net**, not $25; the $25 figure is only the ordinary processing threshold.

## 5. Recipient verification, taxes and disclosures
Stripe-hosted onboarding, not American Rider forms, collects protected identity and bank information. American Rider verifies the returned Stripe account identity, payout eligibility, and any required tax documentation; it never invents completion or holds a real transfer as "paid to bank" absent proper evidence. Stripe setup is a provider process; it is not a legal classification opinion.

A recipient must clearly disclose material paid referral connections in their actual outreach, in accordance with the FTC Endorsement Guides. Participants must not imply that their referral is an employment, insurance or earnings guarantee. Tax status and reporting are reviewed under applicable rules. **IRS guidance establishes a $2,000 Form 1099-NEC threshold for qualifying payments made in 2026**, subject to classification and special circumstances; this does not waive collection of tax information, or automatically establish the correct reporting form. Obtain competent tax/legal review.

## 6. Platform discretion and termination
American Rider may prevent new compensated invites or suspend suspect activity prospectively and retain immutable evidence for disputes. Earned undisputed commissions do not automatically vanish on suspension, termination or inactivity; handle them under the residual and lawful unclaimed-property rules. The program must not promise commissions for a Travel that cannot be funded from American Rider's platform contribution. Describe clear contact and dispute procedures, applicable law and privacy retention in final legal terms.
