# Verified duty and insurance status (2026-10-18)

Already implemented: Operators tap Commence Operations once. Foreground duty renewals run around every 90 seconds, and native background location can renew presence when the mobile OS permits. New dispatch rejects records older than five minutes. This is an engineering grace period, not a legal mandate. A stale record does not cancel an existing Travel. The In Service UI already warns when background tracking is unavailable, but transient network-renewal errors are not displayed there. Improving that notice is recommended.

Insurance: Uploaded policy documents do not prove active coverage. Independently confirmed carrier or broker status starts a 30-day review period. After day 30, the Operator must attest unchanged coverage to use a limited grace period; after day 35, new dispatch remains blocked until independent confirmation. Expiry, cancellation and reductions must block immediately. Periodic status checks reuse stored facts: no new insurer contact or screening company investigation per Travel.
