# American Rider | payment holds, fair cancellation fees and Operator-funded Connect costs
_Review candidate, 19 October 2026. Not an activated live-payment migration._

## What the production source actually does TODAY

- Ordinary Traveler `PaymentIntent` in `backend/payments.js` does **not** request `capture_method:manual`. Stripe therefore captures on successful PaymentSheet confirmation; booking matching requires Stripe `payment.status === succeeded` and charge amount `amount_received` to equal the booking. The Operator transfer is later, at verified completion. This is **not** the same as authorizing funds.
- Traveler cancellation currently authoritatively cancels one identified Travel, refunds captured money when actually paid, and retains the $3 **arrival** fee when `cancelledFrom === arrived`; no proven en-route $3 fee exists.
- Stripe's standard Connect custom pricing on its current public page: $2/month with a bank/debit payout; 0.25% plus $0.25 **per payout**; 0.25% funds routing; optional instant payout 1%. Contractual and account fees must be reconciled to the actual live Stripe billing account. `backend/economics.js` still reserves a 0.5% Connect variable charge and 6c fixed allowance per charged Travel; **those are provisioning assumptions**, not recovered Operator receipts.
- The 20-Travel waiver in `backend/operatorfees.js` was contrary to the later Founder-approved policy. Candidate changes remove the waiver, use a **single month with an actual bank/debit payout** as the trigger, and **require stored explicit agreement BEFORE the payout**. No earlier month's payment can be billed retroactively. The Operator receives 99% of every Travel Fare unchanged. The separately collected $2 cost can incur an additional Stripe card fee (about $0.37 for a $2 domestic collection). Individual payout and funds-routing fee pass-through are **NOT yet end-to-end collected** merely by this policy PR; implementation must avoid duplicate operator debits or subtracting their 99% Fare without explicit separate agreement.

## Recommended fare payment design: prospective, source-only

- **Immediate:** Quote and confirm exact all-in price; authorize card with `capture_method:manual`, ensuring Stripe returns `requires_capture`. Verify amount, identity, actual card scheme expiration (`capture_before`), risk/SCA and timeout *server-side*. Only then dispatch; no Operator may be dispatched on an invalid/expired hold.
- **Scheduled:** Collect and save a permitted payment method through a `SetupIntent`, clearly explaining later off-session authorization and expiry. Approximately **24 hours before pickup**, try authorization. Dispatch only after genuine `requires_capture`. If issuer denies or demands fresh authentication, notify Traveler to repair and never claim confirmed availability or dispatch until restored. This is a design choice, not a guarantee that the card will succeed. Do not double-hold routinely. For changes after authorization, release/re-authorize only when necessary with precise idempotency.
- **Completion:** Capture authorized all-in owed fare with Stripe idempotency, verify `succeeded` and authoritative `amount_received`, then perform normal completion payout once from that captured charge. Failed/uncertain capture -> **no transfer, durable retry/escalation, never treat uncaptured funds as paid**.
- **Cancellation / no car:** Cancel uncaptured hold to release authorization, without a refund event when nothing was captured. If a lawful, separately disclosed cancellation fee is due, capture **only the fee**, up to the original held amount, pay the verified eligible Operator, and release remaining authorization. Payment service can lag in lifting a hold at the card issuer.
- **Grace:** Two minutes after verified Operator acceptance is proposed, but not charged unless real, fresh server-verified approach toward pickup (time/distance and location fresh, reasonable ETA, no Operator fault). A $3 verified-arrival fee already exists. No evidence -> no fee. Display the fee/cancel rules prior to Travel confirmation, and the exact fee upon cancellation. No fee for an unsupplied vehicle, inaccurate/late Operator, or safety/quality/legal exclusions. Uber and Lyft differ by city/class: adopt fairness/evidence, not a false universal industry clock.
- **Long-delay/expired holds:** Stripe generally allows up to 7 days, and actual validity varies by issuer/network/method. Scheduled authorization in a 24-hour window reduces expiry risk; delayed Travel must reauthorize with consent, not auto-capture an expired hold or suppress dispatch. Most non-card payment methods do not support this flow; the eligible card method whitelist needs verification. Stripe webhooks and provider-metadata source of truth, not phone state, authorize each phase.
- **This is NOT released by this PR:** production backend gates, Stripe webhook reconciliation, automated scheduled authorization, tip/payment completion flow, dispatch eligibility, native PaymentSheet display, refunds and e2e Stripe sandbox would all need coordinated transition. Shipping just `capture_method:'manual'` now would break bookings because current dispatch requires `succeeded`.

## Minimal human intervention

- Payment retries, card-auth expiry checks, due scheduled authorizations, reversal and fee reconciliation need idempotent background jobs and rare exception notifications, not Founder approvals for routine service.
- The consent action is a single explicit tap on the existing Operator payouts screen; use Firebase-stored version and time instead of re-asking every month. If non-consenting, do not back-bill. User is not automatically qualified until separate insurance, screening and actual payout readiness are met.
- Once Google paid API and both Stripe webhooks are independently verified on a **new** exact release SHA, retire legacy Render API only when static websites, old TestFlight builds, webhooks, email URLs and account-delete/legal callbacks no longer depend on it. There are four Render static sites and one $7 Starter web service in the current inventory; website hosting is distinct from the paid API. No cancellation by source PR.
- Firebase Authentication sends account address-verification emails (Blaze documented allowance 100,000/day); Resend is instead receipts, insurance email confirmations, family/support. Account check 2026-10-19: 5/3000 monthly and 0/100 daily. Resend Pro $20/mo has 50,000/month without daily cap; enable before verified *Resend* sends exceed 100/day. A hundred new registrations alone is not a proof of Resend cap usage.
