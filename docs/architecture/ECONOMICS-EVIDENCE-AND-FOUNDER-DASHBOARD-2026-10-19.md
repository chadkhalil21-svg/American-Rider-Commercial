## 2026-10-19 incremental audit: actual vendor configuration, first-principles expense scenarios

This section supersedes older hypothetical $2,000 overhead discussions below. No PR can verify a provider invoice or prove commercial net profit. The source pricing engine presently budgets **Stripe domestic card processing at 2.9% + 30c**, foreign-card premium, **99% Fare to Operator**, a **25c contingency reserve** (not an incurred cost), a 25c **operating allowance placeholder** (not measured cost), and a conservatively unverified **0.5% + 6c Connect transfer/payout allowance**. Operator pass-through policy does **not** equal verified pass-through collections. The $2 account fee is separate, payable only for a payout-active month after prior explicit Operator consent; never retroactively bill an unconsented payout. Neither the 25c allowance nor the model's zero-valued unknown external services may be described as actual invoiced cost.

### Supplier inventory / what triggers costs

- **Cloud Run:** priced vCPU-seconds, GiB-seconds and requests, above an account-shared 180k/360k/2m request-based monthly allowance; **egress**, logging/build and any configured minimum instances billed separately. Source scenario assumes request-based, no paid minimum instance and 1 vCPU/1 GiB-equivalent usage. https://cloud.google.com/run/pricing
- **Firestore Standard (Firebase Blaze):** one database free 50k document reads and 20k writes per day; thereafter e.g. $0.03/100k reads and $0.09/100k writes in the cited us-central pricing. Storage, index entry scans, backups and bandwidth require separate meters. Authentication address verification has a distinct Blaze ceiling of 100k/day; this is not a general business email transport. https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/pricing ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/limits
- **Cloud Scheduler:** three jobs free *per billing account*, then $0.10/job per month. Existing planned single scheduler remains inside this allowance when no other project uses it. https://cloud.google.com/scheduler/pricing
- **Legacy Render:** known configured $7/month API plan, still active pending domain/callback and native-version dependency verification. Four static sites are separate; the $7 is an identified plan, NOT hypothetical company overhead or proof of a settled invoice. New model has `legacyRenderApiMonthlyUsd:7`, set to zero only when retired. No new Render subscription assumed.
- **Maps & routing in ACTUAL code:** `backend/place-search.js` chooses a configured **private Photon endpoint**, otherwise **HERE Autosuggest** (HERE may be the primary). `backend/streets.js` uses configured **OSRM**, then OpenTripPlanner. It does *not* call a paid Google Maps service by default. HERE per-call charges/allowances and Photon/OSRM/OTP hosting cannot be accurately computed without the current vendor plan, actual configured infrastructure and call counters; **UNKNOWN, not $0**. Google Maps published $2.83/1k Autocomplete and $5/1k basic route/geocoding above separate 10k/month free caps are only **alternative-provider comparison**, not an American Rider expense. https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing ; https://www.here.com/get-started/pricing
- **Resend:** authenticated account: verified sender **americanrider.app**, sending enabled, receiving disabled, no managed inbox. Free tier 100 sent+received/day / 3k/month, Pro $20/month for 50k with no daily cap. Firebase Auth does **not** create `support@americanrider.com` / `insurance@americanrider.com` mailboxes or deliver general receipts, case updates and insurance discussions. Investigate distinct `.com` domain/mailbox provider and MX records before changes; do not switch DNS blindly. Email and SMS abuse controls needed. https://resend.com/pricing ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/email-custom-domain
- **Other unpriced exposure:** Apple developer enrollment, Google Play, domain/email mailbox, possible Photon/OSRM compute/tiles, HERE toll/route calls, cloud egress/logs/storage, identity/SMS, screening, professional insurance/legal/compliance, customer support and marketing, chargebacks, tax and payment/refund losses. No arbitrary monthly sum is defensible before bills/contracts are collected.

### Payments, cancellations, refunds, and legal caution

The LIVE payment source still **captures** Traveler money at booking, not a temporary hold. Proposed manual-capture flow would authorize at dispatch and capture on completed Travel (or only an eligible, disclosed $3 cancellation fee). **Releasing an uncaptured hold is not a refund**. True refunds remain necessary for already-captured but undelivered, wrongly charged, fraudulent/disputed or otherwise legally refundable payments. Do not delete existing refund support or make an unconditional 'no refunds' promise. Uber documents ~2-minute grace for many economy classes, a progress/late-driver exception, and releasing holds for canceled rides; regional rules vary. Source policy tests are not production deployment. https://help.uber.com/riders/article/review-cancellation-fee?nodeId=6bec690f-ee35-40ba-96ee-c38a8ae796e0

**Engineering:** retain bounded Firestore/geohash candidate discovery pending measured scaling bottlenecks. Uber H3 hexagons and Google S2 spherical geometry are *index representations*, not replacement maps or automatically cheaper dispatch; benchmark location updates, geo-boundary recall, index reads and pickup ETA before migration. Preserve actual-distance ordering and real-time eligibility/revocation checks. https://h3geo.org/docs/ ; https://github.com/google/s2geometry

**Counsel issue (no attorney engaged by this source audit):** Florida Stat. §627.748(7)(d) says TNC-maintained insurance must respond if a driver's coverage lapses or is deficient. Operator-only coverage policy should be evaluated by licensed Florida insurance/TNC counsel before declaring this exposure $0 or claiming launch-law compliance. Also preserve transparent fare disclosures, electronic receipts and lawful consumer reversals. https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/627.748

## What the Founder should read first

1. **$20 example Travel:** $17.50 Fare + $2.50 Platform Fee. The Operator receives $17.33 of Fare (99%, rounded by existing source). Domestic-card modeled contribution = **$1.14** and international-card = **$0.84**, **after** a 25¢ speculative operating-overhead allowance and 25¢ contingency reserve. The 25¢ overhead line is a placeholder, not proof of actual spending.
2. **Transparent cash scenario:** `commercial-economics.js` restores the placeholder 25¢ once, preserves the 25¢ risk reserve, then deducts **all explicitly assumed actual overhead once**, including cloud/doc charges, provider fees and monthly fixed costs. At 95% domestic card mix that gives about **$1.375** room before actual overhead per $20 Travel. This is NOT gross profit, operating profit or a price guarantee.
3. **Break-even is volume-dependent:** Using assumptions of $2,000/month fixed overhead and $0.10 of non-cloud incremental service cost per Travel, the baseline ~50 completed Travels/day is **below** illustrative monthly break-even; ~100/day is above it. Higher costs, taxes, chargebacks or an unquoted TNC insurance obligation can reverse that finding immediately. **Do not advertise profitability.**
4. **Cloud costs:** Render's small baseline web instance is $7/month (0.5 CPU, 512 MB). Google Cloud Run is request-billed with up to 180,000 vCPU-seconds, 360,000 GiB-seconds and 2 million requests monthly under applicable free allowance. Firestore Standard's daily free quota is 50,000 reads/20,000 writes; after quota, illustrative us-central rates are $0.03/100k reads and $0.09/100k writes. Actual production region, billing account, logging, network egress, build, provider and database SKU may differ. Free tiers may already be consumed by **other projects on the billing account**.
5. **GPS scaling:** Each on-duty Operator renewing once per 90s for 8 h generates about 320 daily presence writes; 500 active Operators imply ~160,000 daily writes before a single Travel. That is not a large Firestore bill at unit rates but signals quota exhaustion and database pressure. Preserve the 5-minute stale duty cutoff and 15s safety-critical Travel telemetry; measure cold-start/locked-phone reliability before increasing intervals.
6. **One Founder, one phone:** A daily report should show *completed paid Travels*, *actual Stripe gross and fees*, *verified Operator transfers and unresolved exceptions*, *current Cloud Run/Firestore billed spend* and *risk/legal budget*. Anything unverified should say “unknown,” **never $0**. Make exceptions actionable; routine eligible bookings and payouts automated. A synthetic forecast does not confirm real cash.

## Engineering decisions and further write/read savings

- **Retain bounded geohash matching** now. Merged PR #138 avoids refetching crowded prefix pages, saving 40 document reads per completely full prefix. Uber's H3 is an attractive hierarchical hexagonal index, **not** an instant Firestore pricing reduction. A change would require index migration, spatial boundary tests, live eligibility rechecks and benchmark parity. Defer until measured candidate misses or billing justify it.
- **Use Firestore Query Explain** for geo, pending payout, diagnostic and due-reservation queries. Avoid offset pagination, unnecessary full collections and expensive index entry scans. Do not delete compound indexes used by operations.
- **Possible next optimization:** bound the Operator's lifetime inbox listener server-side *only after* confirming index availability and no older pending offers are lost. Smaller payloads, listener cleanup and stationary-Operator write suppression can help. Avoid sacrificing real-time acceptance, dispatch safety or regulatory evidence for marginal savings.
- **One clock**: Render sweeps off; Google signed scheduler enabled via successful GitHub workflow run 39268828128. Confirm actual post-activation jobs and Cloud Run revision parity; source branch publication does not redeploy existing Cloud Run revision.
- **Unresolved release evidence:** real Apple/Android release, cold-start notification response and locked-phone duty behavior, real payment/refund/payout, and independent TNC insurance legal analysis. These are not established by the green source tests.
