# Proposed issuer-first quote (not activated in published Build 60)

Founder decision: If a Traveler selects a SAVED Stripe card before obtaining the final fare, its verified `card.country` may determine the domestic or international fee. Never trust a phone-supplied country or a card's billing address. Source of truth is the authenticated Stripe PaymentMethod belonging to the current Traveler Customer. A stranger's, missing, unverified or unsupported card must be rejected, not priced at the cheaper US rate. New cards and Apple Pay/Google Pay tokens are selected later inside the current PaymentSheet; until a distinct signed native flow can confirm their actual issuer BEFORE the final price is accepted, retain the international-safe fee and DO NOT claim exact issuer-first price for these methods.

An independently signed native checkout must (a) select saved payment method before quote, (b) send immutable method ID into server quote AND booking, (c) repeat Stripe ownership and issuer check at PaymentIntent creation, (d) bind the PaymentIntent and final all-in amount to that exact card, (e) verify actual authorized card and fee before dispatch/capture, and (f) present a NEW final Total for affirmative acceptance if the traveler changes payment method. Never capture an authorization for a different card from the one that determined a domestic rate; release/requote instead.

This PR now includes signed native-source checkout changes for **previously saved cards**: when the Traveler selects Card (not Apple/Google Pay), the app asks Stripe for its selected saved/default card before quoting; server verifies ownership and issuer and binds the quote, booked fare and PaymentIntent to the same ID; the native SDK confirms that exact saved card; dispatch and manual capture reject a changed card before an Operator is assigned or funds captured. For new cards and Apple/Google Pay, whose issuer is not yet known at quote time in the existing PaymentSheet, current international-safe pricing remains until a later token-first native flow is independently designed and confirmed. The operator receives 99% of the booked Travel Fare regardless. New native source is NOT the published Build 60: signed iOS/Android build, physical device validation and App Review approval remain separate. The source is not merged/activated in production. Source: https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment_methods/object .
