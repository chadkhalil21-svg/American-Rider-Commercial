# ADR 0006 — Fleet economics, monthly active Connect fee, and scale planning

**Decision:** Founder-approved 2026-10-11. **Status:** Forecast code implemented and testable, Operator billing rule approved for **prospective disclosed rollout**, NOT charged under the new rule until authenticated Operator fee consent and complete end-to-end sandbox tests exist. This ADR does not authorize retroactive fee collection.

## Start from the real software, not an invented monthly flat fee

- The canonical `backend/economics.js` for an illustrative **$20 US-card Travel** ($17.50 Fare + $2.50 Platform Fee): Operator receives $17.33; platform earns **$2.67** gross; modeled card charge $0.88, Connect percentage $0.09, estimated $0.06 fixed payout, reserve $0.25, and $0.25 general operating allowance. Historical **$1.14** is a modeled risk-reserved contribution, **not audited corporate profit**. See [full cost audit](../FULL-COST-COVERAGE-AUDIT-2026-10-11.md).
- A company may lose money with zero completed Travels even if **every Travel** has positive contribution; statutory company coverage, service availability, staff and fixed hosting are not automatically proportional to bookings. Owner's goal is positive margin over realistic occupancy/seasonality cohorts, not a mathematically impossible zero-volume profit guarantee.
- 2026-10-11 deployed live map style is [MapLibre/OpenFreeMap](../../src/components/mapStyle.ts), not commercial Google Maps. [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/tos/) is free, allows commercial uses, and has **NO uptime SLA or warranty**. A reliable TNC must budget commercial fallback or owned tiles. **Do not accidentally apply the OSM Foundation tile-server policy to OpenFreeMap**, which is a separate independently operated service.
- The [Stadia Maps](https://stadiamaps.com/pricing/) published commercial tiers are Starter $20/month with 1 million credits then 3c per 1,000 more; Standard $80/7.5 million then 2c per 1,000; Professional $250/25 million then 1.5c per 1,000. The model chooses the least expensive of those **for full paid tile consumption**, not an installed alternative provider. `mapBudgetMode='openFreeMapOnly'` is a zero-tile-invoice, **no-SLA** scenario; `starterStandby` budgets $20/month but **no current failover integration**. Basemap standard vector tiles consume 1 credit; full geocodes typically 20. Model 500 credits/Travel and 400 per on-duty Operator-day as sensitivity, not observed API usage and not a plan purchase.
- The [Resend Pro](https://resend.com/pricing) example is $20/month with 50,000 transactional emails, plus $0.90 per 1,000 overage on paid plans. Model 2 emails per completed Travel as sensitivity. Actual connected Resend account is on a 100/day free ceiling and therefore not commercially provisioned yet. Use reliable push as appropriate, rather than indiscriminately sending email.
- [DigitalOcean Droplets](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets) list $96/month for an 8 vCPU/16 GiB machine. One regional routing instance is a planning line, not proof of sufficient OSRM/OTP CPU/memory or that it is already deployed. Render web capacity ladder is staged, not fixed proportionate user billing.
- [Firestore Standard](https://cloud.google.com/firestore/pricing) us-central1 example: first 50,000 reads and 20,000 writes daily on default DB included, then $0.03/100k reads and $0.09/100k writes. Also index reads, storage, backups and egress are billable. Model 100 reads/40 writes per completed Travel and 360 location writes per active Operator-day. Client GPS permission, data, cellular device costs are borne by device/Operator except company cloud/network consumption.
- [Twilio U.S. outbound SMS](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us) 0.83c per segment + carrier fees; support human 2025 national median hourly wage $21.53 from [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Office-and-Administrative-Support/Customer-service-representatives.htm). $0.17/Travel of **other operations** budgets SMS, toll/POI fallback, human-support case expectation, API egress/observability and routine review, excluding above fixed map/email/Firestore and the separate reserve. This **is a scenario allowance, not invoices**.
- Following [Stanford GSB spatial-price research](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/spatial-pricing-ridesharing-networks), measure **real demand** and operator distribution; never infer Travels from registered Operators alone. [HBR Hagiu/Wright on marketplaces](https://hbr.org/2024/07/will-that-marketplace-succeed) emphasizes two-sided density, which makes registered supply distinct from realized transactions.

## One bounded founder-operable forecasting model

Deterministic `backend/fleet-economics-forecast.js` is management-only (NEVER used to quote, transfer, authorize, bill or admit a market). Base workload: **60% active** of registered Operators, **20 working days per active Operator per month**, **5 completed Travels per working day**, **6 on-duty hours/day**, **20 standard bank payouts monthly per active account (daily rolling default if not changed)**. **Four weekly payouts were previously only a scenario and must not be presented as American Rider's actual payout schedule.** That is 60 completed Travels/month per registered Operator, or *2 per registered Operator per calendar day* over a 30-day month. It is an assumption, not a historical forecast for Miami. **All modeled $20 Travels have US cards**; overseas card and refund mix worsen results.

Funding: $2.67 platform Travel revenue + $2 active-account recovery (assumed 100% successful) per monthly active payout account. Expense ledger: domestic cards $0.88/Travel, Connect percentage $0.09/Travel, Connect account charge $2/active payout account, fixed payouts $0.25 each, $0.25/Travel **risk provision** (not Stripe fee), other variable services $0.17/Travel, on-duty cloud/processing **budget** $0.035/Operator-hour, commercial mapping fallback priced by credits, Resend paid email budget, Firestore billed document example, staged Render and router/monitor capacity; **company-wide corporate/admin/insurance/legal/safety planning placeholder** $1,500/month plus $3 per registered Operator. This last placeholder is **not** a signed insurer quotation and may substantially underestimate lawful TNC company coverage, permits, 24/7 response and accounting. Real taxes, credits/refunds beyond reserve, charge failures, paid demand acquisition, hardware, marketing, app-store and disputed losses are scenario-sensitive. `collectionFraction` models nonpayment.

**Accounting consistency:** A platform that fully collects the active account charge must record BOTH the $2 from the Operator in gross receipts and the $2 charged by Stripe as an expense. They cancel in the pre-tax remainder, but cannot simply disappear from an 'all expenses' table. The displayed totals include both sides, assuming successful collection, not zero spend or extra profit.\n\n**No universal profitability guarantee:** At 50 registered Operators and only 10 active, completing 2/day × 20 days each, the 400-Travels/month scenario loses about **$1,421** under the same budget. At the normal 3,000-Travels base but with company fixed budget **$5,000** instead of $1,500, the 50-Operator scenario loses about **$1,799**. These are essential adverse checks, not defects to hide.

The map fallback and paid email lines are included in the expense budget even though the service may in the future choose to self-host or contract for different terms. **Never double-count** these plus a purported per-Travel blanket $0.25; this model replaces the existing overhead allowance with named categories and retains the $0.25 loss reserve as a provision.

## Corrections established in the next actual-code review (2026-10-11)

- **Daily is the safe default forecast.** `backend/payments.js connectAccountFor` creates US Stripe Express accounts without setting a payout interval. [Stripe connected-account payout docs](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/payouts-connected-accounts) say balances normally pay out on a **daily rolling basis**, and the platform can change Express automatic schedules. A 20-day active Operator may therefore receive up to 20 standard payouts/month, not the prior four-weekly assumption. At **25¢ fixed per payout**, daily (20) = **$5 per monthly active Operator**; weekly (4) = **$1**. The difference = **$4 per active Operator per month**. Payout count depends on actual balance dates, settlement delays and Operator settings, so verify individual account evidence before changing the schedule. This is a material expense correction, not a feature released to production. The percentage payout and funds-routing costs remain independently modeled at 0.5% aggregate; instant 1% plus any ordinary applicable costs must be borne by the voluntarily opting Operator with transparent consent.
- **HERE is not yet a verified 'rare paid fallback.'** `backend/tolls.js` bypasses HERE for an OSRM no-toll route only if `region.tollCoverage.certified === true`; `backend/regions.js` defines a `tollPolicy.status:'verified'` but currently **no** `tollCoverage.certified` for South Florida. As implemented, the toll-pricing path **can attempt HERE even on no-toll OSM routes** when a HERE key is set, and fail closed when none exists. [HERE's toll guide](https://docs.here.com/routing/docs/routing-v8-tolls-for-route) says requesting tolls adds a transaction. We need actual per-quote attempt counters, market-authority toll road certification, then the already-coded safe bypass; **never claim the documented intention is fully deployed**, and never simply set `certified=true` to save costs. Track as [issue #121](https://github.com/chadkhalil21-svg/American-Rider/issues/121).
- **MapLibre != tile provider.** MapLibre is free renderer; `src/components/mapStyle.ts` currently hardcodes OpenFreeMap vector tile and font URLs. Stadia is not yet installed as failover, although the renderer is provider-neutral. Budget outage-resilience separately. Map style comparisons, API license terms, caching/attribution and actual map tile counts must be measured before commitment.
- **Solo founder is not $0 regulatory costs.** The previous $1,500/month plus $3/registered Operator corporate budget was an explicit illustrative placeholder, NOT a supplier invoice and NOT proof the founder has staff or corporate insurance. The model can rerun with these values set to zero **only to show a technology-and-operations ceiling**, clearly excluding unpriced statutory/contract/legal/tax and owner labor. An unknown company liability cannot be declared nonexistent because the operator pays the operator's own policy.
- **10,000 and 50,000 Operators** have no validated production hosting capacity tier. The model accepts explicit externally researched hypothetical `tiers`, e.g. by scaling the 1,000-Operator assumed compute budget 10×/50×. These scenarios do **not** certify CPU, GPS throughput, market-region count, company compliance or net profit at those fleet sizes.

## $2 active Stripe Connect account charge — new settled policy, safe migration

The founder explicitly approves eliminating the **20-completed-Travels/month waiver**: an Operator should reimburse the **$2 Stripe monthly active payout-account charge once per qualifying month**, regardless of 1, 19, 20 or 100 completed Travels. An Operator who has **no bank/debit-card payout in a month** owes **$0** under this policy. The 99% of Travel Fare promise remains unchanged; this is a separate transparently disclosed pass-through, **not a 1% commission change**, and neither American Rider nor Stripe should call it a per-Travel fee.

Vendor authority: [Stripe Connect](https://stripe.com/connect/pricing) charges platforms using their own pricing $2 *when a connected account has a payout to bank/debit card in a month*, plus 0.25% + $0.25 each payout and a separate 0.25% funds-routing charge. Current `backend/operatorfees.js` instead **waives $2 after 20**; current `backend/payments.js` charges monthly off-session to saved card, grossed up to $2.37 (US card) or $2.41 (other/unknown card) to recover the additional card fee; with a card charge, the pass-through only happens on successful payment. No payout = zero charge. This is already one idempotent charge keyed to UID/month/amount, and its sweep records failed collections for retry; do not run duplicate billings.

**Safe implementation sequence, NOT yet completed by this research PR:** (a) update Operator agreement and payout screen in all five supported languages with this **once-per-payout-month** disclosure, (b) obtain verifiable affirmative acceptance of the revised fee terms, including off-session payment authorization or Stripe balance debit consent, effective prospectively (never retroactive), (c) verify an eligible lower-cost [Stripe account debit](https://support.stripe.com/questions/debit-a-connected-account?locale=en-GB) is possible on our exact Connect setup and only then consider its **1.5%** fee (about $0.03 for $2), otherwise reuse the existing card-charge method with gross-up, (d) adapt monthly fee sweep to pay/no-pay and remove the monthly completed-Travels query, (e) sandbox test 0/1/19/20/100 Travels, account with/without payout, accepted/declined terms, insufficient Stripe balance, duplicate webhook, off-session card failures, refund, retries, time-zone/month boundary, collection loss, localization and 1M-Operator bounded processing, (f) exact-head release gates and signed device/Founder review. Until consent + integration work is finished, **do not silently change live billing of existing Operators**. This policy decision is approved; collection under the new rule is NOT.

## 2026-10-11 no-waiver implementation stage (draft, fail-safe)

The new accepted policy has no ride-count waiver for a connected account with at least one payout in the applicable month. `backend/operatorfees.js` includes a consent-gated no-waiver calculation, and skips completed-Travel queries once valid consent is recorded. Legacy accounts retain their original 20-Travel waiver; zero-payout months remain $0. A signed, authenticated Operator agreement acceptance and effective-month writer **does not yet exist**, so the new terms are **not live**. Do not invent consent by changing a Firestore document or environment variable; do not charge retroactively. Before merging a live billing change, implement and prove authenticated server-written agreement consent, all five localized Operator disclosures, explicit recovery method authorization, first eligible payout accounting where Stripe permits it, exact monthly idempotency/failed-recovery behavior, live money flow unchanged in sandbox, negative balance tests, month boundaries, duplicate/replayed webhooks, and twice-run exact-head CI. Fee display preferred: **Stripe payout services** and **$2 once in each month you receive a payout**; show any other payout fees separately and before choice. A $2 Operator charge is not the 0.25% + $0.25 Stripe per-payout fee or 0.25% routing fee. See Stripe pricing: https://stripe.com/connect/pricing and Apple's transparent-fee design: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles/.
