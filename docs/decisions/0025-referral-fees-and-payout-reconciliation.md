## Verified public Stripe mechanics
Stripe's platform-handled pricing lists $2 **once per connected account in a month when any bank/debit-card payout is sent**, NOT $2 per payout, plus 0.25% + $0.25 per payout, 0.25% funds routing on bank payout volume, and 1% Instant Payout. Automatic payouts can trigger monthly activity. Stripe invoices platform charges in aggregate at month end, so a first payout event is eligibility evidence, not itself a verified Stripe monthly invoice. Source: https://stripe.com/connect/pricing and https://docs.stripe.com/connect/margin-reports .
