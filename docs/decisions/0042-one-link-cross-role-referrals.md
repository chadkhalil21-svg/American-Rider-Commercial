# ADR 0042 — One referral link, both roles, one safe reward ledger (source-only proposal)

**22 October 2026. Status: PREPARED FOR REVIEW; not implemented in production and not approved compensation terms.**

## Founder objective
Grow Travelers and Operators through current Operators, Travelers and independent recruiting partners. One Founder operates from one phone, with no recruiting subscription, separate app, extra Operator qualification step or high-frequency Firestore polling. This ADR is a proposed extension of existing systems, not evidence of functioning links, legal approval, cash payouts or commercial onboarding.

## Closed loop, without six separate systems
There are **two invited outcomes**: an eligible **Operator** or an eligible **Traveler**. The inviter may be a Traveler, an Operator, or an approved independent partner. An existing American Rider account can participate in more than one capacity; the referral benefit is determined by the *invited outcome*, not a parallel account type. A verified account may have at most one credited inviter *for each invited role*, and never itself. When an existing Traveler later qualifies as an Operator, Operator attribution is possible only before Operator activation under the same rules. No recursive/multilevel commissions. No rewards for recruiting a recruiter.

## Founder UX refinement after 22 October live-device screenshots
The current Traveler screen at `app/invite.tsx` is already an elegant **unpaid, untracked** native share message. It intentionally removed a deceptive referral code and a fabricated $10 invitation reward on 17 September; do not reintroduce claims before verified settlement. The Operator drawer (`src/components/operator.tsx`) does not yet provide Invite. **No UI action in this source-only PR.**

**Treat normal Travelers as customers, never advertise or label them as recruiters.** Preserve existing calm screen spacing, type, preview and standard native Share sheet. Proposed Traveler-facing label: **Share American Rider**; secondary small **Show QR code** control for nearby voluntary scans, using exactly the same personal link as Share. An explicit native share sheet lets the user choose contacts or messaging apps on their own device; **American Rider must not read/upload their address book, require the invitee's phone number, or ask an Operator to solicit passenger details.** An Operator may use the same Share/QR function, potentially showing either invitation destination, with an understated separate earnings/accounting detail. Partner-specific cash and tax dashboards must not appear in ordinary Traveler UI.

**Invited person must be NEW to the invited role:** a currently registered, eligible Traveler cannot be claimed as a 'new Traveler' by another Operator or independent partner, whether through another share link, QR, app reinstall, or existing booking. A currently commissioned Operator cannot be claimed again as a new Operator. An existing Traveler who later genuinely starts Operator onboarding may qualify as a new Operator-role invitee **before** their Operator role is activated, once, only after review. Capture accepted attribution once server-side; don't count a claim from simply opening the link. No incentives for rebooking existing Travelers under new invite codes.

**App-store-first public landing, not another marketing website:** one verified `https://americanrider.app/join?r=...` URL is the identity of the invite. Installed app opens instantly where OS verified association permits. If not installed, use a minimal fast same-domain redirect to the actual iOS App Store/Google Play listing (once published), with an accessible **reopen invitation** recovery path. The tiny fallback page is only for safe OS/store routing and disclosed referral context. Apple universal links do NOT inherently carry referral state through first installation; optional Smart App Banner may help resume a browser context, Android Play Install Referrer can assist after native validation. No fake promise of frictionless iOS deferred deep linking, fingerprinting, harvesting email/phone, paid install attribution vendor or separate subscription. **No public store listing is verified active at this review; don't enable public invites that direct to a nonexistent store entry.**

**Text truth:** Existing shared message `I travel with American Rider, a national transportation service` must be rechecked at launch because new signups may not yet have traveled and public booking geography is not nationwide. Proposed accurate default: **Discover American Rider.** Add ordinary service-area information where necessary. A visually subtle but **clear** paid-invitation disclosure should accompany any incentivized endorsement/link/QR (FTC). An opaque QR cannot itself convey a disclosure; show it immediately alongside the QR/landing, not buried only in general Terms.

**Referral cap proposed by founder's recent discussion:** **$0.15 for each of up to 200 genuine paid completed Travels, maximum $30 per newly eligible invitee**, regardless whether the invitee joins as Operator or Traveler. Roles determine award delivery: Operator referrer cash credited separate from existing 99% fare Stripe settlement; ordinary Traveler referrer matched Travel credits (requires new verified price-reduction funding path; no additional Connect account); approved independent partner cash via separately vetted low-frequency provider payout. This is a **proposed ceiling, not yet approved/implemented in money systems**. One actual Travel can give rise to both a referred Operator reward and a referred Traveler reward: reserve up to **$0.30 total** jointly from platform margin **before** offers are publicly promised. Refund/dispute prevention and worker/tax review remain hard gates. Commission rate, market, award expiry, eligible role and credit restrictions must be transparent and agreed in advance.

**Channel conduct:** Social/contact sharing to existing friends with consent is fine as a product flow; do not direct Operators to market during active passenger service on competing platforms. Published Lyft guidance advises drivers not to request rider contact information; Uber prohibits off-platform pickups. Operator obligations under each competing platform's contract need a real attorney's review, not a claim of formal counsel consultation. The program rewards only future lawful American Rider activity, not redirecting or canceling an active competing-platform ride.

No outside counsel was directly retained/contacted.

## One link; zero new steps for the invited person
- An inviter sees **Invite an Operator** / **Invite a Traveler** sharing the same opaque, non-PII invitation ID and a role target in the URL. Independent partners receive the same site-based link. QR is only another representation of the URL; SMS/share sheet is primary.
- Use the existing domain `americanrider.app/join?r=OPAQUEID&role=operator|traveler`. **Not an active URL yet.** Link landing explains the role, shows one Continue action, and explicitly discloses any compensation. No names, phone numbers or trip histories in the URL. No Firestore writes on link clicks.
- Existing installs: Apple Universal Links / Android App Links require hosting exact verified AASA and assetlinks.json plus native app entitlements, signed build, and physical device tests. Current `app.json` contains only the custom `americanrider` scheme, not the required universal/app-link association. Never claim browser installation preserves an invite by magic.
- New installs: the website can direct to the store. On iOS the user may need to reopen the original invitation after installing because Universal Links do not by themselves defer attribution across installation; offer a visible, optional **Reopen invitation** fallback. Android Play Install Referrer could later support permitted post-install attribution, but requires native configuration and verification. Do **not** use Firebase Dynamic Links; discontinued in August 2025. Avoid fingerprinting.
- After authenticated signup or subsequent Operator role choice, server checks link's *server-owned* inviter identity and atomically fixes first eligible referral for the target role. Never trust an inviter UID supplied in the URL. Existing `app/operator/qualify.tsx`, insurance/screening/vehicle/Stripe onboarding remain completely unchanged and eligibility-gated. A referral cannot bypass geographical availability or compliance.

## Reward model, initially OFF
- No money/credits are paid for clicking, downloading, registering or unverified referrals. Only an authenticated settled paid Travel with verified participant identity can create a potential reward.
- Proposed Operator recruit reward is **15 cents per qualifying Travel for the first 200, max $30**, drawn only from verified platform contribution, never Operator's 99% Fare. Earlier $50 milestone ideas are superseded as preferred starting design. Not a live or legally approved award; confirm the per-Travel reward budget first.
- Proposed Traveler-recruit award is the same **15 cents per qualifying Travel for the first 200, max $30 per new Traveler**, with the *inviter's* role deciding delivery. Ordinary Traveler inviter receives properly funded in-app Travel credits rather than a new bank/Connect account; Operator inviter and vetted independent partner may receive cash separately through approved payout paths. Do not give the existing Traveler another new-signup gift. Credit redemption must be built into and funded within the existing authoritative quote/payment path, not silently deducted from the Operator's 99% Fare.
- Every Travel can legitimately have both a referred Traveler and a referred Operator, so two 15-cent rewards may be due **on one Travel ($0.30 total)**. Both must draw from **one joint per-Travel budget** after Stripe processing, risk reserves, overhead and a retained platform contribution approved in advance. Do not advertise a fixed reward that the economics cannot reliably fund. Terms, market eligibility, caps, and refund treatment must be published before activation.
- Exact immutable reward identity: `programVersion + travelId + invitedRole + referredUid`. One source of truth, one idempotent settlement event, no cross-Travel double awards. Credits/payouts are released only after capture, refund/dispute hold and available funding. Refunds, chargebacks, reversals and consent revocation require clear compensating ledger entries, not silent deletion.
- Program may be OFF by market and role. Payments should be accumulated; monthly payouts above a reviewed minimum reduce external transfer fees. Reuse Stripe only after separate recipient onboarding, payout, bank, tax reporting and actual per-recipient fees have been validated. Operator Stripe payouts do not automatically prove a Traveler/recruiter is eligible to receive cash. Do not put recruiter transfers on the Operator's 99% fare transfer path.

## Cost and abuse constraints
- Never write a document for a QR scan or landing page view. One immutable claim per accepted role attribution, once per qualifying captured Travel; use fixed event keys for replay. Store short non-sensitive summary on user's existing profile where appropriate and avoid copying entire trip objects to recruiter views.
- Do not watch every Travel in realtime per recruiter, update a hot shared counter, scan old journeys repeatedly, or poll Firebase for referral progress. Read bounded, indexed pages only on opening the recruiter summary; monthly job works from immutable events only. Avoid unused field indexes and unbounded timestamps.
- Server rejects self/related identities, duplicate/competing claims, existing Operators/Travelers outside eligibility, suspended inviters, fraudulent/fake rides, canceled, refunded, disputed and synthetic Travels. Abuse rules must cover shared payment methods/phones/devices only in a privacy-preserving verified server process, not speculative fingerprinting.
- Identity of invited Traveler/Operator and all trip origin/destination/payment records stay private. Recruiter sees only aggregate counts and reward status, not identity, trip traces or bank information. Age/guardian rules forbid monetized Teen referrals by default. Paid recommendation disclosure is mandatory wherever relevant.
- Do not add Uber H3: its hexagonal grid is spatial technology and irrelevant to identity referral tracking. Preserve existing geohash dispatch architecture and ADR 0034.
